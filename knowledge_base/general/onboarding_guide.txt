Partner Onboarding Guide

New partners begin onboarding by completing their administrative profile setup in the Partner Portal.

The second step is domain and email verification, which must be completed before accessing integration features.

The third step is connecting the partner's CRM system using our native integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, or Slack).

New partners are entitled to a 45-minute live training session with a Customer Success specialist.

A complete video knowledge base is available in the Partner Portal for self-paced learning.

Partners must complete onboarding within 14 days of account activation. After this period, access to setup wizards expires and manual configuration is required.

For onboarding issues or questions, partners should contact their assigned Customer Success representative or email onboarding@senseiassistant.com.
