Account Support

Customers can update their account information (name, email, phone number) through the Account Settings page.

If a customer requests an email change, they must verify the new email address before the update takes effect.

Account deactivation can be requested by contacting support. Once deactivated, the account data is retained for 30 days before permanent deletion.

If a customer wants to reactivate a deactivated account within the 30-day window, they should contact support with their registered email address.

For security reasons, account ownership transfers are not supported. If a customer needs to transfer their subscription, they must cancel and create a new account.

If the customer reports unauthorized access to their account, advise them to reset their password immediately and enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). Escalate the case to the security team.

For questions about discounts, loyalty programs, special pricing, or promotional offers, inform the customer that a human support agent will follow up to review their request. Do not confirm or deny the existence of any discount or promotion.
